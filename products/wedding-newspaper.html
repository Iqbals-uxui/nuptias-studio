    Before you order

    Common questions

    How many copies do we need?

    One per guest, not per household. Unlike an invitation, people read these separately during drinks and take them home. Add ten spares for the photographer, your parents and yourselves.

    We're not writers. Will it sound naff?

    That's exactly what the £45 and £145 options are for. The most common failure is a paper written in wedding-speak — "we are so blessed to share our special day". We write it as a newspaper would: specific, dry, and about actual events. Specific is always funnier than sentimental.

    How late can we leave it?

    Eight weeks is comfortable. Six is doable. Under four we would need to talk, and we may have to drop the crossword, which is compiled by hand and takes the longest.

    Can we include a section you haven't listed?

    Yes, and the best ones usually aren't on the list — a recipe from a grandmother, an obituary for a much-missed dog, letters to the editor written by your friends in advance. Ask.

    What happens if a photograph is too low-resolution?

    We flag it before proofing and either place it smaller, treat it as a deliberate grain effect, or ask you for an alternative. Nothing goes to print looking like a mistake.

    Does the QR code cost extra?

    No. We generate it, point it at whichever shared album you're using, test it on both iPhone and Android, and print it at a size that actually scans across a dim room.
