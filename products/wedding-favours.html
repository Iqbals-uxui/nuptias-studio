    Before you order

    Common questions

    Do you supply the Zam Zam water itself?

    No — we supply the empty bottle, the personalised sticker and the tag. You fill them yourself, which keeps the water in your hands rather than ours. Most couples fill the bottles the week before, and it goes faster than you would think with two people and a funnel.

    How many should I order?

    One per guest, not per household — favours go home individually. Add around 5% as spares for breakages and for guests who take a second for someone who could not come.

    Should I choose flat-packed or assembled?

    Flat-packed is cheaper and fine if you have willing family. Be realistic about the numbers though: threading and tying 300 tags takes an evening between two people. At 400 or more we would recommend paying the £0.35 and getting your evening back.

    Can you set Arabic calligraphy?

    Yes, in-house, including bilingual English and Arabic layouts. We send a proof of the Arabic specifically for you to check before printing — we would much rather you spotted something than us guess at it.

    Can each favour have a different guest's name on it?

    Yes, but it is priced separately because it means variable-data printing and a great deal more checking. Send your guest list as a spreadsheet and we will quote it. Most couples find a shared design plus separate place cards works out better.

    What happens if some arrive broken?

    Glass is packed in fitted trays and breakages are rare, but send us a photograph within 48 hours and we will remake and resend the affected favours at no cost.
