    Before you order

    Common questions

    Can I have my names and date on the sticker?

    Yes — that is the point of them. You type your text when you choose a design, and we set it in the design's typeface. Two lines are included; more is fine, just tell us in the notes.

    What sizes do you do?

    37mm, 45mm, 51mm and 60mm. 37mm suits a 50ml bottle, 45mm suits a favour box lid, and 51mm or 60mm work as small signs or on larger jars.

    Can you set Arabic calligraphy?

    Yes, in-house, including bilingual English and Arabic layouts. We always send a proof of the Arabic specifically for you to check, because we would much rather you spotted something than us guess.

    What is the minimum order?

    One hundred stickers. Prices start at £0.55 each and fall to £0.32 each at 500 or more, so larger celebrations get considerably better value.

    Will they stick to glass bottles?

    Yes. Our stickers use a permanent adhesive that holds on clean, dry glass, plastic, card and kraft paper. They are not designed to be repositioned once applied.

    Can I use them for something other than a wedding?

    Certainly. We print for Nikah, Walima, Mehndi, engagements, Eid, christenings, baby showers, birthdays and small businesses. The design process is the same.
