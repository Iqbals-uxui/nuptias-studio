    Before you order

    Common questions

    How many invitations do I need?

    One per household rather than one per guest, since couples and families share. Add about ten spares for late invitations, keepsakes and your photographer. For 100 guests that is usually around 60 to 65 suites.

    What is in one suite?

    The invitation card, a matching RSVP card, an envelope to post it in and a second envelope for the RSVP to come back in. Detail cards are an optional extra.

    Can you print guest names on them?

    Yes, for £0.30 per suite. Send your list as a spreadsheet with one household per row and we typeset every name in your chosen typeface. Corrections are free up to proof approval.

    Do you do Nikah and Walima invitations?

    Yes. The Nikah gold and Islamic geometric designs are built for it, and we set bilingual English and Arabic layouts where neither language reads like a translation of the other.

    I do not know what to write. Can you help?

    Yes. We send a wording guide covering traditional, modern, blended-family and Nikah phrasing, and we will adapt any of it to your circumstances at proof stage at no extra cost.

    When should I order?

    Four to six months before the wedding, with RSVPs due about eight weeks before the day. Remember the three week production time sits inside that.
