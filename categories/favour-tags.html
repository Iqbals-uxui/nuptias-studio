    Before you order

    Common questions

    Does the price include twine?

    Yes. Pre-cut jute twine is included as standard and supplied loose alongside the tags. Satin ribbon is a small extra, and we can thread everything through for you for £0.10 per tag.

    Can I have a message on the back?

    Yes, for £0.12 per tag. A du'a, a verse, a thank-you or the order of the day — anything that fits. It is printed at the same time, so there is no extra lead time.

    How long does threading take if I do it myself?

    Be realistic: threading and tying around 300 tags takes an evening between two people. At 400 or more we would suggest paying the £0.10 and getting your evening back.

    What is the minimum order?

    Fifty tags. Prices start at £0.45 each and drop to £0.32 each at 500 or more.

    Can the tags match my invitations?

    Yes, and they should. Tell us which invitation design you have chosen and we will carry the same typeface, artwork and ink across to the tags at no extra cost.

    Do you do tags with individual guest names?

    Yes, but it is quoted separately because it means variable-data printing and much more checking. Send your guest list as a spreadsheet and we will price it.
