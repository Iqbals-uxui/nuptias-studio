    Before you order

    Common questions

    Can I buy just the box, or just the jewellery?

    Yes. They are priced and sold separately with no minimum on either. Plenty of people buy boxes for the bridesmaids and one piece of jewellery for the maid of honour — that is a normal order, not an awkward one.

    Can each box have a different name on it?

    Yes, and it costs nothing extra. Set the quantity to the number of boxes you need and list each name and role in the notes box. We engrave each lid individually and check the spellings with you on the proof.

    Is there a minimum order?

    No. Order one if you want one. The unit price drops at four and again at eight, which is where most bridal parties land.

    Are these only for bridesmaids?

    Not at all. We engrave them for maids of honour, mothers of the bride and groom, flower girls, and as thank-yous for whoever did the heavy lifting on the day. The lid text is entirely yours.

    Will the jewellery fit inside the box?

    Earrings, pendants, bracelets and hair pins fit the keepsake and trinket boxes. The single and double ring boxes are sized for rings only. If you want them paired up, choose the packaging option on the jewellery and we will place it inside before it ships.

    How long does it take?

    Three weeks from proof approval, the same as everything else we make. Engraving is the slow part, not the boxes themselves, so ordering eight takes no longer than ordering one.
