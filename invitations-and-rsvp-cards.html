    Wording, sorted

    The bit most couples find hardest. We send all of this with your first proof — you don't have to work it out alone.

    Who hosts? Traditional, modern and blended-family wording
    • Traditional, where the bride's parents host: "Mr and Mrs Ellis request the pleasure of your company at the marriage of their daughter…"
    • Modern, where the couple host: "Together with our families, we invite you to celebrate our wedding."
    • Blended families, naming all parents without it running to four lines.
    • Remembering a parent who has died, warmly and without derailing the tone.
    How to word an adults-only wedding without offending anyone

    Before you order

    Common questions

    How many invitations do I actually need?

    One per household, not one per guest — couples and families share. Then add about ten spares for late invitations, keepsakes and your photographer. For a 100-guest wedding that usually lands around 60–65 suites.

    Can you print guest names on the invitations and envelopes?

    Yes — £0.30 per suite for the invitation and £0.40 for the envelope. Send your list as a spreadsheet with one household per row and we typeset every name in your collection font. Name corrections are free up to proof approval.

    What if I order 50 and then need 15 more?

    Re-orders are charged at your original unit price with no new setup fee, as long as the design is unchanged. Realistically, order the spares up front — a top-up of 15 costs more in postage than in printing.

    Can I mix formats — some A5, some DL?

    Yes, but they're priced as two separate lines. Couples usually do this for a small number of A5 suites for close family, with DL for everyone else.

    Will the RSVP card fit standard postage?

    The A7 RSVP card in its envelope posts at the standard letter rate. An A5 invitation suite with a wax seal will need a large letter stamp — we tell you which rate applies before you print.
