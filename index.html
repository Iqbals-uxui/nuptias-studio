    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    How long will my order take?

    Three weeks from the moment you approve your proof. Your first proof arrives within three working days of ordering, so allow around four weeks in total from first contact. If your date is tighter than that, ask — we can sometimes work faster, but we would rather tell you honestly than let you down.

    What details do you need from me?

    At minimum, your names exactly as you want them printed and the date of your event. For seating plans we need your guest list as a spreadsheet; for favours and stickers we need any wording or Arabic text you want included. Every product on this page collects these before it goes into your basket.

    Do you make Nikah and Walima favours?

    Yes — it is one of the things we are known for. Personalised 50ml Zam Zam water bottles with kraft tags and Arabic calligraphy stickers, date boxes and sweet jars. Orders of 300 to 600 favours are routine, and the unit price drops considerably at those quantities.

    Can you set Arabic calligraphy?

    Yes, in-house, including bilingual English and Arabic layouts. We always send a proof of the Arabic for you to check before printing, because we would rather you spotted anything than us guess.

    Is the basket a checkout?

    No. It builds an estimate you send to us, and we come back with a firm quote and an invoice. Nothing is charged automatically, and nothing is made until you have approved a proof.

    What if a guest drops out after I have approved the seating plan?

    Changes are free right up to proof approval. After approval the plan goes into production, so a reprint is chargeable. We recommend approving your seating plan no earlier than three weeks before the day.

    Do you deliver, and what about breakages?

    Tracked UK delivery as standard, free over £150. Acrylic ships with rigid backing and corner protection. In the unlikely event something arrives damaged, send us a photograph within 48 hours and we will remake it.
