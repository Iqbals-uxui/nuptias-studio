Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long will my order take?

Three weeks from the moment you approve your proof. Your first proof arrives within three working days of ordering, so allow around four weeks in total. Plain bottles with no personalisation ship within one week. If your date is tighter, ask — we can sometimes work faster, but we would rather tell you honestly than let you down.

Can I order just bottles, or just stickers?

Yes. Every category is sold separately with its own minimum — 50 bottles, 100 stickers, 50 tags, 30 invitation suites. Nothing is bundled unless you want it to be. That said, adding stickers or tags while you are ordering bottles is cheaper than ordering them separately, because it shares one design setup.

What details do you need from me?

Your names exactly as you want them printed, and the date of your event. For stickers and tags we also need the text you want on them. Everything on this site collects these before it goes into your basket.

Do you supply the Zam Zam water?

No — the bottles come empty and unsealed with the caps loose, so you fill them yourself. Most couples do it the week before, and it goes faster than you would think with two people and a funnel.

Can you set Arabic calligraphy?

Yes, in-house, including bilingual English and Arabic layouts. We always send a proof of the Arabic for you to check before printing, because we would rather you spotted anything than us guess.

Is the basket a checkout?

No. It builds an estimate you send to us, and we come back with a firm quote and an invoice. Nothing is charged automatically, and nothing is made until you have approved a proof.

Do you deliver, and what about breakages?

Tracked UK delivery as standard, free over £150. Glass is packed in fitted trays. In the unlikely event something arrives damaged, send us a photograph within 48 hours and we will remake it.
